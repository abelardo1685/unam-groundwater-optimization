# Optimization in Groundwater — Graduate Course (UNAM)

Teaching materials for the graduate course **Optimization in Groundwater**, Posgrado en
Ciencias de la Tierra, Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM).

**Instructor:** Dr.-Ing. Abelardo Rodríguez-Pretelín · Instituto de Geología, UNAM
· ORCID [0000-0002-3560-2410](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3560-2410)

## Scope

The course introduces optimization methods for groundwater management and protection:
single- and multi-objective formulations, metaheuristics (particle swarm, OMOPSO),
optimization under uncertainty, and applications to pumping design and wellhead
protection.

## Contents

The material is organized in units (slides, notes and literature). See the unit folders
and the accompanying references.

## Related open-source tools

- [pyOMOPSO](https://github.com/abelardo1685/pyOMOPSO) — parallel multi-objective PSO used in the course.
- [rrwpt](https://github.com/abelardo1685/rrwpt) — transient probabilistic wellhead protection area delineation.
